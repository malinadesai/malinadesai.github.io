Data pulled from MAST via astroquery.mast.Observations. “Confirmed” = proposal has at least one executed, calibrated exposure (calib_level ≠ −1). “Scheduled” = proposal is approved and visible in MAST but has not yet been observed — treat these as planned, not archival, data.
